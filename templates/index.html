Algorithm Architecture

How the Recommendation Engine Works

Transparent, mathematical content-based filtering without opaque black-box AI.

Step 1

Profile Document Construction

The system combines your qualifications, technical skills, interests, and target roles into an enriched candidate profile text document.

Step 2

TF-IDF Vectorization

Scikit-learn’s TF-IDF Vectorizer tokenizes n-grams (unigrams & bigrams) and converts text into numerical vectors, weighting rare technical skills higher.

Step 3

Cosine Similarity Calculation

The model computes the angular cosine distance between the candidate vector and every benchmark job vector in the dataset, determining geometric proximity.

Step 4

Skill Gap Roadmap Extraction

Candidate competencies are intersected with each job's required skills to extract matched skills and generate a targeted learning roadmap.
